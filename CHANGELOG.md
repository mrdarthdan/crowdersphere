# Changelog

Each release's notes are taken from its section here, so they show on the GitHub release page and in the app's **Update available** window. Add a section for the new version before releasing.

## 2.4.0

- **Videos** — the new **Video** button adds a YouTube or Vimeo link, which WordPress shows as a player, or a video file from your computer (MP4, MOV or WebM), which is uploaded to your Media Library with the post. You can also paste a video link on an empty line, or drag a video file onto the page.
- Videos sit in the post like pictures: align, size, caption, move and delete them from the same toolbar.
- CrowderSphere warns you about iPhone HEVC videos that many browsers can't play, and shows how far along a big video upload is when you send the post.

## 2.3.0

- **Welcome tour** — a one-minute tour on first launch, and a **Welcome to CrowderSphere** sample post to experiment on.
- **Help menu** — the new **?** button at the bottom left takes the tour again, opens the [Getting Started guide](https://github.com/mrdarthdan/crowdersphere/blob/main/docs/getting-started.md), or adds a fresh Welcome post.
- **Mac:** keyboard shortcuts (new post, save, link, zoom) now use **Cmd** instead of Ctrl.

## 2.2.0

- **New app icon** — redrawn so it's sharp at every size, from the taskbar to the Mac dock.
- **Version 1 cleanup** — on Windows, installing or updating now removes the old version 1 (Setup.cmd) program files that were left behind, and fixes old shortcuts to open the new app. Your posts and settings aren't touched.

## 2.1.0

- **Automatic updates** — CrowderSphere checks for a new version when it starts and offers to install it. Turn this off or check by hand in **Settings → Updates**.

## 2.0.1

- **Export to Word** now includes pictures hosted on other websites.
- Opening CrowderSphere while it's already open brings up the existing window instead of starting a second copy that could overwrite your post.

## 2.0.0

- CrowderSphere is now a desktop app for **Windows, Mac and Linux**, with a new editor under the hood. Your posts, settings and WordPress connection carry over from version 1.
